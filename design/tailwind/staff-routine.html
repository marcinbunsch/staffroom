Every Monday morning, pull the state of the hiring pipeline from Ashby and the recruiting channel in Slack. For each open role, report: candidates in each stage, anything that has not moved in more than seven days, and interviews scheduled for the coming week.

Write it as a short brief — no more than one screen. Lead with what needs a decision from me, then the numbers. If a role has had no new candidates for two weeks, say so plainly and suggest one thing to change. Never include compensation figures in the shared version; keep those in the confidential appendix.

Send it to me by 07:30. Do not post it to the channel without my approval.
